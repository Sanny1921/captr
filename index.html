FAQ

Questions, answered

What is clipping?

Clipping means cutting your longer content (podcasts, streams, videos) into short, feed-ready clips. We edit each clip for the hook and watch time, then distribute it through creator pages where short-form content lives.

How is that different from influencer marketing?

Clipping distributes your content through creators. Influencer marketing has creators make or feature content about your brand in their own voice. Most campaigns mix both. Tell us your goal and we will recommend a split.

How much does a campaign cost?

It depends on the scope: how many creators, what kind of content, how long the campaign runs. Email us your brief and we will come back with a plan and a quote.

How fast do you reply?

Within 24–48 hours on email.

I’m a creator. Is joining free?

Yes. Applying is free. We review your profile and reach out when a campaign fits your content.

Do creators get paid?

Yes. Creators are paid for the campaigns they accept. Exact terms come with each campaign brief.
