Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

CAPTR ("we") is a clipping and influencer marketing agency based in Patna, India. This page explains what we collect through this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Creator applications: the details you type into the join form - name, social handles, follower and reach numbers, city, languages, past collaborations, WhatsApp number and email - plus the insights screenshots you upload.
  • Brand enquiries: whatever you include when you email us.

How we use it

  • To review creator applications and match creators to brand campaigns.
  • To reply to brand enquiries within 24–48 hours.
  • To contact you about campaigns on WhatsApp or email, only when you have asked us to or applied.

Where it is stored

Creator applications are stored in the agency's own Google Sheet, and uploaded screenshots in the agency's Google Drive. Access is limited to the CAPTR team.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not share your details with brands until we discuss a specific campaign with you.
  • We do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Your choices

Want your application or data removed? Email captrmarketingagency@gmail.com and we will delete it.
